Our Story

Crafted with Care, Roasted with Passion

Hearth & Bean began as a passion project between two friends who believed that great coffee should bridge the gap between farm and cup. We source our beans directly from small-scale farmers within 200 miles of our roastery, ensuring fair wages and sustainable practices at every step.

Every batch is roasted in small quantities in our wood-fired drum roaster, developed over weeks of cupping and tweaking until we achieve the perfect balance of flavor, aroma, and body. From the earthy richness of our Dark Roast to the bright clarity of our Light Roast, each variety is a testament to the craft of specialty coffee.
